Restaurant Services

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and an undersized or failing heater reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temperature dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater working alongside the main supply. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. If the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize at peak service. Our technicians verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and log every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

Your schedule comes first. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. When the work can't happen during service, we install after hours, and we keep downtime to a minimum so the kitchen opens on time.

Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect venting and draft, confirm proper combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and following through on our recommendations helps keep small issues from becoming liabilities. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

Preventive Care

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

A maintenance program from Winchester Water Heater is built around your equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. On each scheduled visit, we handle tank flushing and anode checks, descaling of tankless heat exchangers, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and gas valve inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the tap.
