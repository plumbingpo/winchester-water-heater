Common Problems

When Homeowners Should Call for Help

Most failures start small and give you clues first. Below are the warning signs local homeowners run into most, and any one of them is reason enough to call us for a closer look.

Hot Water Gone Cold

When the heater is on but the water stays cold, the fault is almost always one specific component. We find it and fix it fast.

Same-day repair details

Water Around the Heater

A dripping temperature and pressure relief valve is a warning sign, not a nuisance. It usually means excess pressure or heat, often from a missing expansion tank.

Water heater leak repair

Rumbling Tank, Rusty Water

A rotten-egg smell in the hot water usually comes from bacteria reacting with the anode rod. Swapping to a different anode and flushing the tank typically fixes it.

Maintenance & flushing

A Heater Near the End

When repair bills start stacking up, a new, more efficient heater often costs less over time than keeping the old one limping along.

Replacement options

Tankless Error Codes

Hot-cold-hot water, a unit that shuts down mid-shower, or a flashing code usually points to mineral scale. A proper descaling often solves it.
