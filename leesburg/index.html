Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Leesburg

A little routine care makes all the difference between a water heater that lasts and one that fails early. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a yearly flush, an anode rod check every three to five years, a yearly test of the T&P valve, a temperature setting around 120 degrees, and annual descaling for tankless units on hard water, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

The age of your heater matters just as much. With regular maintenance, a standard tank typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like discolored hot water, rumbling, or a damp spot under the tank, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and is far less stressful than an emergency swap with water spreading across the floor.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Take a spent anode rod: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but if nobody replaces it, the water starts eating the tank steel, and just like that a minor fix has become a full replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and spares you a surprise cleanup.

Typical Water Heater Issues in Leesburg

Well before a tank floods a Leesburg garage, it almost always shows warning signs. Watch for a popping or rumbling sound during heating, rust-colored hot water, a sulfur smell from the hot tap, moisture around the base, or hot water that runs out a little sooner each month. These are early signs of heavy sediment, a depleted anode, a weak element or burner, and early corrosion, and caught early, they're usually an affordable repair or a planned replacement. Ignored, they end in a ruptured tank and a cleanup bill. Pay close attention to a relief valve that drips or discharges: it's the tank telling you pressure or temperature is running too high, and it needs a professional look soon. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so the small problem never gets the chance to grow.
